# One More? 0.16.1 — Characters and stored food

- Main menu, tutorial and dealer events use the restored transparent-background dealer illustration. The dealer retains asynchronous head, card-hand and drumming-hand motions. Other event characters use the simplified flat SVGs; completed events show their character beside the recap, while route choices use prop icons. Original character studies and both minimal dealer revisions are retained.
- Pan's intervention has no portrait or explanatory captions: a hand brings in the glass, the glass contains the blast and breaks, and the bomb returns to the shuffled pile. The unskipped full-motion presentation is approximately six seconds. The ring sits on a finger rather than on the palm.
- Thermos stores one actual food card for the next table. It leaves the current table score immediately; storage is not consumption and creates no residue. Its identity, compatible enchantment, current transformation and single-card bonus survive the transition. A stored temporary card remains temporary. A paired card leaves its partner unpaired without granting that partner a second pairing this table.
- Added cold storage (3 banked points to start next table with a selected food from the deck), a new-menu exchange (4 points, three replacement foods, compatible enchantment retained), and a closing meal (two permanent foods for a displayed existing pledge). They use the standard run's route selection and normal draft flow.
- The six proposed narrowly conditional pledges were withdrawn. They do not appear in new runs, reward pools or the collection. Definitions remain only to avoid invalidating an existing local preview save. Existing approved cards and pledges remain available.
- Food-cost waivers appear as a dedicated icon and remaining-use count. The redundant zero, lower-left explanation and persistent bomb counter have been removed. Explicit full-motion settings take priority over the browser's reduced-motion preference; reduced motion remains available in settings.
- The standard local game entry is `http://127.0.0.1:8888/`. Independent development fixtures are not required to access the new cards, art or events. The web release includes 302 passing automated checks; Windows and itch.io builds are separate uploads.
