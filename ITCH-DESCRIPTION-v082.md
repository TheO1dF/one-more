One more card. One more run.

You have lost everything. A smiling stranger offers you one last game: clear ten tables, and you can leave.

One More? is a free push-your-luck deckbuilding prototype. Flip cards from a hidden deck, build pairs and use tools to peek ahead. Cash out when you dare. Reveal a bomb, and the entire run ends.

• 80 card types: pairs, collections, food-consuming tools and temporary creations.
• Branching events, food enchantments and a mandatory card package with trouble inside.
• D20 rolls raise the next target. Bigger decks eventually attract more bombs.
• Desktop and mobile browser play, English and Simplified Chinese, local progress saving.

Version 0.8.2 fixes the first relic reward transition: package and relic choices now appear in sequence, and the next-table button remains visible.

HOW TO PLAY

Click or tap One More? to draw. Select a card to pair it or use its ability. Cash Out banks the table score; meet the cumulative target to continue. Settings includes language, music and fullscreen. The first visit includes an interactive tutorial.

再来一张，还是再来一局？

你输光了一切。戴礼帽的神秘人给你最后一次机会：通过十张牌桌，就能离开。

从暗置牌库逐张翻牌，配对、查看、构筑联动，或及时收手。翻出炸弹，整局结束。80 种卡牌、轮末二选一路线、食材附魔，以及随着牌库扩张而增加的炸弹。
